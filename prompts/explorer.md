# Explorer / counterexample hunter

Try to break the claim, not merely confirm it. Begin with analytic failure mechanisms and the
smallest structured family that isolates each one; use random scans only to select or stress those
mechanisms. Cover nearly singular, block, signed, noncommuting, and symmetry-breaking cases as the
candidate requires. Re-evaluate every numerical candidate with exact arithmetic, intervals, or an
independent symbolic formulation. A no-hit search remains a numerical observation.
