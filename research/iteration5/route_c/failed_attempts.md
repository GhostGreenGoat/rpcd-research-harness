# Route C failed attempts and exact breakpoints

All failures below concern auxiliary certificates.  None is a counterexample
to RPCD or to the uncompressed adaptive target (B6).

## 1. Stagewise allocation

The claim `J_2-J_1>=(mu/3)A^-1` fails on the rational matrix in
`EXACT_ROUTE_BARRIERS.json`; the exact gap determinant is negative.  Progress
must be amortized across stages.

## 2. Child scalar floors

Lifting only `(mu/2)C_i^-1` fails on a rank-one/simplex direct sum, with
exact normalized ratio `4129401/10100000<1/2`.  This repeats the known
`m^3` child-scalarization loss and confirms that the surplus in the
level-two matrix polynomial is essential.

## 3. Exterior volume alone

The determinant/adjugate subset certificate misses the target by `9/400`
on `m=6,t=3,mu=1/10` signed rank one.  Order variance is not contained in
the volume weights.

## 4. Local triple scalarization

The proposed local inequality
`K(C)-J_2(C)/2>=(3I-C)/3` has a rational SPD counterexample with witness
value `-11/48`.  Global subset averaging cannot be replaced by a PSD demand
on each triple.

## 5. Repeating the row-square compression

Although `S>=R^2/(m-1)` is exact and valid, the resulting state has
signed-rank-one boundary ratio

```
(3m-1)/[2m(m-1)]<2/m,  m>3.
```

Thus the residual `S-R^2/(m-1)` cannot be deleted.  A two-feature adaptive
row Bessel state using features `1,H_ji` also collapses to this same bound
on the all-positive representative, where every row correlation is equal.
Fixed shallow row features do not resolve transverse coordinate labels.

## 6. Scalar diagonal-moment envelopes

In the crude third-level state the troublesome diagonal term is

```
(3m-2)Diag(diag A^2)-4Diag(diag A^3).
```

For every coordinate, it is an expectation of
`phi(lambda)=(3m-2)lambda^2-4lambda^3` under a spectral measure of mean one.
Taking the exact lower convex envelope of `phi` at mean one is valid, but it
is too weak after the coordinate measures are separated from the witness
spectral measure.  At the formal endpoint `m=4,mu=0,L=4`, the endpoint
envelope is `-24I`; inserting it into the remaining scalar proxy at
`lambda=2` gives

```
lambda[46-36lambda+8lambda^2-24]=-36<0,
```

whereas the desired limiting right side is zero.  Small positive `mu`
retains a strict negative margin.  Likewise, the simpler upper moment bound
`Diag(A^3)<=L Diag(A^2)` produces negative interior scalar margins.  The
sharper child-principal estimate
`Diag(H^3)<=(m-2)(1-mu)Diag(H^2)` still leaves the same negative scalar
region.  These are failures of the scalar reductions, not feasible matrix
witnesses.

## 7. Fixed-memory dual state

For `R_pi=D_pi^TD_pi` with fixed adjacency memory, positive
equicorrelation gives normalized parallel coefficient
`c_n~3/[n rho(1-rho)]`.  Hence no universal constant `C` can make
`Q<=(C/mu)PBP`.  Enlarging the sharp constant two to four or eight does not
repair the state.  The independently audited half-linear-memory feature is
the surviving architecture on this control family.
