# Phase-3 bibliography

Literature status is separate from mathematical verification and priority.
No novelty or priority claim is made for the RPCD reduction.

1. Huzihiro Araki, *On an inequality of Lieb and Thirring*, Letters in
   Mathematical Physics **19**(2) (1990), 167--170.
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01045887.
   Claim used: for positive semidefinite `A,B`, exponent `r>=1`, and `q>=0`,
   the Araki--Lieb--Thirring trace inequality has the direction
   `tr[(B^(1/2) A B^(1/2))^(rq)] <=
   tr[(B^(r/2) A^r B^(r/2))^q]`.  The route uses only `r=p`, `q=1`,
   `A=C_j`, and `B=H_j`.

An accessible restatement checked during this pass is Koenraad M. R.
Audenaert, *On the Araki--Lieb--Thirring inequality*, arXiv:math/0701129
(submitted 2007), https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0701129, Theorem 1.  It is not
used as priority evidence; it was used only to cross-check the exponent and
inequality direction of Araki's theorem.
